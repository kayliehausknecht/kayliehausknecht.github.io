Kaylie Hausknecht

I am currently a PhD student in physics at MIT interested in biophysics and statistical physics. I am also a Student Researcher on the Science AI team at Google Research.

I graduated with an A.B. in physics and astrophysics from Harvard University in 2024. From 2020-2022, I interned at NASA, first in the Computational Fluid Dynamics group at NASA Langley Research Center and then in the Data Science Group at NASA Ames Research Center.
